About this site

We are a group of conservation biologists and orangutan conservation researchers committed to orangutan conservation. Our aim for this website is to present information on orangutan conservation available in one location. This site will always be a work in progress and therefore incomplete. We welcome ideas on other data and information that would be useful to include. The site was designed by Serge Wich with input from Borneo Futures, Hutan and Wildlife Impact.
